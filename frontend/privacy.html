Privacy notice

This notice follows Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and its 7 principles. The demo uses synthetic CVs only. Please do not upload a real person's CV.

1. General principle

Sign-in is optional. Without it, Varity works with a token that stays in your browser. With it (Replit sign-in), Varity stores, per account: your Replit user id, email, display name, profile image link, and the times you created the account and last signed in. Per profile it stores your skill list, your target role and your pathway ticks. It also keeps the cleaned CV text (emails, phone numbers and ID numbers already replaced) so the CV builder can use it. It does not keep the CV file. We use this only to show your own routes and pathway.

2. Notice and Choice principle

You choose whether to sign in. Before you upload a CV, you tick a box to confirm it is a sample CV. The "Try an example" button needs no CV. When AI is on, the CV text goes to Anthropic (Claude) for reading. Before the first project review, you agree in a box that your repo files go to the AI review jury.

3. Disclosure principle

The only outside party that sees CV text is Anthropic (Claude), and only for reading the CV when AI is on. Code first redacts email addresses, phone numbers and ID numbers. A name can still remain in the text.

Project reviews: when you submit a repo, Varity reads it from GitHub and sends the files it picks for each checklist item to the review jury: Anthropic (Claude), and OpenAI (GPT) and DeepSeek models through the KrackedDevs gateway. Only public repos can be reviewed. Nothing else from your profile is sent. Cert checks read your Credly badge page from Credly. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

4. Security principle

Data sits in Replit PostgreSQL (a SQLite file if no PostgreSQL is set). The session cookie is signed, HttpOnly, and lasts 30 days.

5. Retention principle

The CV file is not stored, and the cleaned CV text is deleted when you delete your profile. Evidence quotes are cut to the sentence around each skill, but a name in the same sentence can remain. Accounts with no sign-in for 12 months are deleted, with their profiles, projects, reviews and certs.

6. Data Integrity principle

You check every skill against its quote before you save, and you can untick or remove any skill. Varity never keeps a skill you did not keep.

7. Access principle

You can see your saved profile on the CV and routes screen. To delete it, use "Delete my profile". To delete your account and profile, send DELETE /api/me (the account chip offers this when you are signed in).
