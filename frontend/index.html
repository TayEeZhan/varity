Varity prototype. Signals and skill momentum are output of the AI Trend Signal Layer (run 20261022T050908-ab9c76); if the server cannot be reached, the page shows a dated offline snapshot and says so. CVs are read by the skill profile intake: every skill is checked against a quote from the CV. The job forecast comes from the role forecast engine (Hacker News hiring posts). Match scores, routes, the pathway and today's stops are computed by code from your saved profile and the files in data/; example profiles are synthetic. Scenarios are estimates. Varity supports your judgement; it does not replace it.
