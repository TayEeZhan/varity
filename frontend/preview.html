Varity prototype. Signals and skill momentum are output of the AI Trend Signal Layer; if the server cannot be reached, the page shows a dated offline snapshot and says so. CVs are read by the skill profile intake: every skill is checked against a quote from the CV. The job forecast comes from the role forecast engine (Hacker News hiring posts). Example profiles are synthetic. Scenarios are estimates. Varity supports your judgement; it does not replace it.
